    How to play

    Click one of your hands, then one of the computer's. Their hand gains as many fingers as yours has. Counts wrap past 4, so 4 + 4 leaves 3; exactly 5 and the hand is dead. (The cutoff rules kill any hand that reaches 5 or more.)

    Instead of attacking you can split: share your fingers out again. Splitting onto a dead hand brings it back. A split that empties one hand is a suicide split.

    Lose both hands and you lose. If the game reaches the move limit, it's a draw.
