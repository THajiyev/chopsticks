    How to play

    Drag one of your hands onto one of the computer's, or click yours and then theirs. Their hand gains as many fingers as yours has.

    Counts wrap past 4: 4 + 4 leaves 3. Exactly 5 and the hand is dead. (Under the cutoff rules, any total of 5 or more kills.)

    Instead of attacking you can split: share your fingers out again. Splitting onto a dead hand brings it back. A split that empties one hand is a suicide split.

    Lose both hands and you lose. A game that reaches the move limit is a draw.

    Changes take effect when a new game starts.
